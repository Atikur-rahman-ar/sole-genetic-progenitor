\title{\bfseries Sole Genetic Progenitors: The Endowed-Pair Model\\
of Single-Couple Human Origin\\[6pt]
\large A feasibility theorem for an $N=2$ founding couple without a
pre-bottleneck population}

\author{Atikur Rahman\\
\small Independent Researcher, Dhaka-1206, Bangladesh}

\date{\today}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\noindent The strongest version of the single-couple human origin (SHCO)
hypothesis claims that a founding couple of $N=2$ individuals can be the
\emph{sole genetic progenitors} of a human population: the source of all of
its heritable variation, requiring no pre-existing gene pool. The standard
diversity objection to this claim --- that two individuals cannot carry the
variation of a species --- assumes that the couple's genomes are a random
sample of an external reservoir. This paper removes the assumption. We
introduce the \emph{endowed-pair
(EP) model}, in which the couple's genomes enter as an initial condition:
their four gene copies per locus carry a divergence (endowment) $h$ bounded
not by ancestry but by the heterozygosity of natural primate genomes
($3.1$--$4.1$ million SNVs in humans, $5.7$ million in chimpanzees;
\citealp{PradoMartinez2013,Auton2015}). The EP master equation,
$E[T_2] = D_{<c} + e^{-\Lambda_c}\bigl[T_0 + \tfrac34 h/(2\mu)\bigr]$,
replaces the sampled model's pre-population term with the endowment's
$\tfrac34$-weighted coalescent-equivalent depth ($1.23$ Myr at the
canonical endowment).
A generation bound prices what the endowment must do: mutation accumulated
below $T_0$ can add at most $2\mu T_0 = 0.53\times10^{-3}$ per site ($53\%$
of the observed diversity, $1.6$ million SNVs per founder), so every
trajectory of the class requires $h \ge \tfrac43(\pi_{\mathrm{obs}} - 2\mu T_0)
= 0.63\times10^{-3}$ ($1.9$ million SNVs per founder) --- the couple must
\emph{transmit} standing divergence, and the theorem quantifies how much.
At the canonical point ($T_0 = 550$ kya, growth factor $1.6$, plateau
$\Ne^{\max} = 3\times10^4$, $h = 1.576\times10^{-3}$) the model reproduces
observed nucleotide diversity to $0.01\%$ ($\pi = 1.000\times10^{-3}$),
satisfies the autosomal coalescence-depth range, yields Tajima's $D =
-0.93$ (observed $D$ of order $-2$ in large-sample series), and reproduces
the observed short-range linkage-disequilibrium profile ($E[r^2] \approx
0.30$ at 1 kb, within the observed $0.3$--$0.5$ band). Under sole
progenitorship the four-lineage compression time TMR4A equals $T_0$
identically --- matching the direct-clock estimate $495 \pm 100$ kya of
\citet{Swamidass2019,Swamidass2022} --- so the diversity-feasibility
window and the independent TMR4A window coincide: $T_0 \in [400, 595]$ kya,
the regime in which independent backward simulation also finds a
single-couple origin compatible with the data \citep{HossjerGauger2019}.
The uniparental MRCAs are the couple themselves (their published date
estimates being anchored-clock estimates that require the ancestral-rate
correction $s \approx 2$ of \citealp{Scally2012} --- a conditional,
corroborative layer, the window itself resting on autosomal evidence
alone); the bottleneck is
invisible to PSMC/MSMC-type reconstruction; and balanced-locus retention
($2.1$--$2.5$ of 4 founder lineages) and regeneration ($8{,}705$ candidate
HLA-scale lineages in 550 kyr) answer the allelic-supply half of the
polymorphism objection, the deep-root lineage count being its stated
open per-locus test. Archaic
admixture is handled by the \emph{descendant-clade extension}: the
Neanderthal and Denisovan lineages branch from the couple's descendants, so
the introgressed $2$--$6\%$ of non-African genomes is couple-descended DNA
returning through the archaic relatives --- full nuclear coverage holds at
$T_0 \in [550, 595]$ kya, and the model predicts the archaic branches' low
heterozygosity (an order of magnitude below the modern branch), their deep
cross-coalescence with moderns at $T_0$, and their extreme internal
bottlenecks, all as observed; the archaic clades' uniparental compartments
are the one scoped exception, falsified clock-free as couple-descended and
assignable only to replacement from outside the couple's descent
\citep{Mendez2016,Posth2017} --- so sole progenitorship is a claim about
modern genomes, not about the archaic clades' uniparental compartments.
The provenance of the endowment is the theorem's stated boundary: a couple
carrying $h = 1.576\times10^{-3}$ --- the coalescent-equivalent of a
population of $\Ne \approx 3.3\times10^{4}$ --- may be sampled from such a
population, constituted de novo, or assembled from diverged ancestral
lineages; all three readings generate identical descendant genetics, and
no diversity measurement distinguishes them. All quantities are emitted by
a single fixed-seed reproducibility script; every rate the model reads is
pedigree-measured, every correction it applies is a published
reconciliation, and its one free parameter is bounded by genomes that
exist. The falsification conditions are stated in advance and are,
without exception, measurable quantities. The diversity and structure of
the human genome do not falsify a sole-progenitor couple at
$T_0 \approx 550$ kya; what the theorem establishes is underdetermination
under exact bounds --- the diversity objection fails as stated, the
endowment's provenance is beyond the reach of any diversity measurement,
and restoring the objection now requires a conjunction of assumptions ---
a mandatory pre-population, a persistently order-of-magnitude-lower
plateau, the refusal of the published ancestral-rate correction --- that
no diversity measurement can test. The claim long dismissed as
genetically impossible is, on every measurable quantity, the position the
data leave standing.
\end{abstract}
